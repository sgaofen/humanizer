1/ one year into my PhD and honestly the biggest shock wasn't the workload, it was realizing NOBODY actually knows what they're doing. we're all just googling stuff and hoping for the best lol

2/ also nobody tells you how much of research is just... being confused. like 80% of my time is "wait why isn't this working" and 20% is briefly understanding something before getting confused again

3/ i thought my advisor would hand me a clear roadmap. instead it's more like "idk figure it out" energy 90% of the time. terrifying at first, weirdly freeing now

4/ reading papers used to feel like homework. now i catch myself reading a paper for fun on a saturday and immediately question my life choices

5/ failure is just... the whole job? like my experiments fail constantly and somehow that's considered normal and even useful. nobody warned me my main skill would become "being okay with things not working"

6/ but also the small wins hit different. one tiny result that finally makes sense after weeks of nothing = pure serotonin. worth it? ask me again in year 3
