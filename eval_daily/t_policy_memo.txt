Memo to the Transportation Committee regarding the proposed bus lane on Harbor Avenue.

The pilot ran from March through August on the 1.4-mile segment between Third Street and the bridge. Average bus travel time through the segment fell from 14 minutes to 9, and on-time performance for Route 22 improved from 61% to 84%. Ridership on the route rose 12% over the same period, though some of that increase likely reflects the seasonal pattern we see every summer.

Car travel times increased by about two minutes during the evening peak and were unchanged at other times. We received 213 public comments: 140 in favor, 58 opposed, and 15 mixed. Opposition concentrated among businesses on the north side who lost 22 parking spaces.

Staff recommend making the lane permanent, with two changes: restore parking on the north side outside peak hours, and extend the lane one block east to the bridge approach, where buses still merge into general traffic. The estimated cost of the changes is $310,000, which is within the existing capital budget.
